Quasi-hereditary Ordering Calculator for Nakayama Algebras

A Nakayama algebra is a finite-dimensional algebra whose indecomposable projective and indecomposable injective modules are uniserial: their submodules are linearly ordered by inclusion. This tool considers basic connected algebras A = KQ/I over an algebraically closed field K, with Q a linearly oriented or cyclically oriented quiver and I an admissible ideal generated by paths.

For a total order ≼ on the isomorphism classes of simple modules, the standard module Δ(i) is the largest quotient of the projective cover P(i) whose composition factors S(j) satisfy j ≼ i. The order is quasi-hereditary if every Δ(i) is Schurian (EndAΔ(i) ≅ K) and every indecomposable projective module admits a filtration by standard modules. This tool computes the number q(A) of such total orders.

Method: the minimal-relation criterion and X-set recurrence. Integers and fractions are exact; percentages are rounded. All computation takes place locally in your browser.
